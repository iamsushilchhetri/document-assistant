Palm Mind AI is a technology company that develops artificial intelligence solutions for businesses. The company focuses on machine learning, natural language processing and data analytics.

The AI and ML internship requires knowledge of Python programming, basic statistics and machine learning algorithms. Candidates should also understand databases and REST APIs.

Interns work with experienced developers to build practical AI applications. They are responsible for data preprocessing, model evaluation and backend development.

Interviews are conducted from Monday to Friday between 10 AM and 4 PM. Candidates must provide their name, email address, preferred date and time to book an interview.

The internship provides opportunities to develop technical skills, gain industry experience and collaborate with other developers.
